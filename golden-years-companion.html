Golden Years Companion — Complete Edition

The calm home companion for Mom or Dad — designed by a nurse, set up once by family

Her tablet sits in a drawer. You live two states away, worrying.

The apps confuse her. The video calls never quite happen. Her medicine times blur together, and every unanswered phone makes your heart drop. Golden Years Companion was built for exactly this: a calm, kind companion that runs on the tablet or phone she already has — set up once, by you, in about thirty minutes — that she simply lives with from then on.

What it is: a voice-first web companion with big buttons and a patient design. No new gadget to buy, nothing to install, and no monthly fee to keep it alive.

What's inside

  • Morning Welcome — a warm spoken hello: her name, the day, the weather, and what's on today.
  • My Medicines — gentle medication reminders with Taken and Snooze taps; a missed dose is flagged in the Family View so you can check in. If she is ever unsure which pill is which, the Companion's only answer is: ask your family or your pharmacist.
  • Check-In Circle — a daily "I'm OK" hello you can see in the Family View, and a big help button that keeps family numbers on screen, ready to tap.
  • Call Family — a wall of familiar faces. One tap on a face starts a video call. No menus, no passwords, no numbers to remember.
  • My Appointments — one big "Join my appointment" button opens the doctor's own visit link you pasted in, with a calm getting-ready checklist and a card of today's medicines and questions to show the doctor. The visit itself happens entirely in the provider's own system.
  • Music & Memory Lane — songs from her golden years, big-type lyrics, and our own Tlott12 recordings. Music for comfort, memory, and mood.
  • CalmMind Daily — five quiet minutes of guided breathing, gratitude, and evening wind-down listening from our calm library.
  • Tell a Story — her memories, spoken or written, kept on the device and gathered into life chapters the family can read and answer.
  • Meals for the Week — simple, kind meal ideas with read-aloud recipes and a one-tap grocery list.
  • BrainSpark Games — two gentle games for a pleasant few minutes. No clock, no pressure, no scores that judge.
  • Family Papers — a simple organizer: what important papers exist, where the originals live, and who to call. An organizer to help you find things — not a legal record.
  • Family View — your side of the Companion: one-sitting setup, today's check-ins, and the medicine log.

What this product is — and is not

Golden Years Companion is a companion and organizer — not a medical device, not an emergency service. It does not detect falls and does not identify medication. In an emergency call 911. Designed by Ting Lott, RN — reminders and check-ins only, always honest about what it is.

One honest limit, in plain language: the Companion runs in the web browser, so its reminders and check-in hellos can only sound while the app is open on the device. The included START HERE guide shows the family how to leave it open, like a bedside clock. No health outcome and no income result is promised, implied, or guaranteed by this product.
