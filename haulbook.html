The paperwork your ELD never touches

You already run a registered ELD because the law requires it. What the ELD never does is the rest of the paperwork: proving how long a shipper kept you waiting, knowing what a load really paid per mile after the deadhead, finding your per-diem days at tax time, rebuilding miles by state for IFTA.

So drivers stitch it together — a back-office subscription here, an IFTA report fee there, a spreadsheet for per-diem, another for detention. A typical one-truck setup runs about $25–50 a month, every month, forever.

HaulBook replaces that stack's paperwork for $59. Once.
