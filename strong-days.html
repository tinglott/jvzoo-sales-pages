Strong Days — Small Habits, Real Change

A nurse-designed, faith-friendly habits course for building habits that survive real life — by Ting Lott, RN

Small steps. Honest restarts. No shame.

You've started before. You've stopped before. And every guru on the internet made it sound like the stopping was a character flaw.

It wasn't. You were given a system built for people with quiet lives, full sleep, and no anxiety — and then blamed when it didn't survive your real one.

Strong Days was designed by Ting Lott, a psychiatric nurse of 17 years who spent his career helping people change in the hardest rooms of their lives — and it is built on the discovery he made there: people don't change because they're shamed into it. They change one small, kept promise at a time.

  • Why willpower fails — the nurse's view of cues, cravings, sleep, and stress, and what actually works instead.
  • Start absurdly small — tiny habits and habit stacking that fit inside a real, tired day.
  • Design your surroundings — environment over motivation, so the right choice gets easier.
  • Habits for the anxious mind, and sleep as the keystone habit — calming routines for racing-thought evenings.
  • Breaking a habit that holds you — compassionate and addiction-informed, where a lapse is data, never failure.
  • The Restart Plan — the honest plan for missed days that every other system leaves out.

How it works in three steps

Strong Days, the small-habits system without shame works in three simple steps:

  1. Step 1: Start absurdly small — one tiny habit, stacked onto something you already do every day.
  2. Step 2: Track it in the app and the workbook, with a weekly review that plans for your worst week, not your best.
  3. Step 3: When a day slips, use the Restart Plan. A lapse is data, never failure — and the 21-day Restart Without Shame Challenge starts again with you.
