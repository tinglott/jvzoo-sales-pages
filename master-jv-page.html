Approved Claims — What You May and May Not Say

You may say:

  • "Nurse-designed" — the products are designed by Ting Lott, RN, a psychiatric nurse of 17 years. That is biography, and it's true.
  • What a product actually contains (lessons, files, app features) — from the product's own sales page.
  • Your own honest experience with a review copy.

You may never say:

  • Income or earnings claims — for these products or for promoting them.
  • Health outcome claims — no product here diagnoses, treats, cures, or prevents anything; never promise sleep, healing, safety, or clinical results.
  • That a health app detects falls, identifies pills, checks drug interactions, or alerts 911 — they do not claim these features.
  • Anything about the vendor's credentials beyond the line above (17 years, psychiatric nursing).

People come first in all ARTISY copy — person-first language, always (people living with a condition, never a diagnosis as an identity).
